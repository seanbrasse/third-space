# The Stolen Lantern

Elsie’s missing light now leads to a finite shared side quest: ask at Reed pond, hear Pip’s account, enter Lantern Cave, recover the lantern from the plinth inside the Lantern Hollow, and return it to Elsie. Any admitted member can complete the next shared step. The lantern belongs to the home rather than a player’s hotbar. Every canonical member present in the home’s membership at completion, including offline members, earns a personal two-apple thank-you. New members after completion can see the resolved story but receive no retroactive reward. Full pockets leave earned apples pending at Elsie.

This is a separate milestone built on PR33’s repaired living-world loop. It retains those pursuit, direction and responsive HUD fixes. Native voice stays OFF behind the existing gates. No engine migration, generated Meshy asset, provider activation or new dependency is included.

## What to play

- Meet Elsie at the pond south of Bramblewick, choose Ask about the missing lantern, then speak to Pip at Copperbutton camp. The journal and current conversations name the next destination. The completed side quest stops replacing Pip/Lumen/Morrow’s still-active keeper or Fair Rind guidance.
- The existing cave mouth at `(64,81.5)` is now a natural doorway. The ninth interior is an original 18×16 cavern with shallow pools, supplies, bedrolls, eight safe arrivals and a down-arrow exit. Walk to `(9,5.5)` inside and press E or touch the recovery marker. When anyone recovers the one lantern, its plinth loses the lantern in every recipient’s view; the rock remains. Exit via the existing walk-to-exit control or doorway.
- Two visible hostile goblins patrol the cave road and reed pond. They can target eligible civilians or exposed players near any active human, independent of cameras. Initial warning and a fixed 900ms strike circle allow escape. After a civilian casualty, they may turn on exposed humans for at most six seconds with fresh warnings, then visibly return. Light, fire, watching, seats, respawn protection, wards, LOS, life/zone revisions and leashes are rechecked. Defeated goblins respawn after 45 seconds; no quest requires grinding them.
- Lumen’s free ward lasts 20 seconds with a 60-second cooldown. It clears Morrow’s snare, prevents another while active, and repels these goblins within four tiles, protecting nearby civilians too. It is not protection from every horror encounter. At night, Morrow telegraphs a fixed snare circle for 1.2 seconds; dodging, light, safety or a ward cancels it. A successful snare slows outdoor forest movement to 65% for five seconds. Pulses have a 20-second cooldown. These effects never stack or renew on rejoin; absolute deadlines persist. Steady rings and HUD countdowns remain readable with reduced motion.
- A verified knife hit on Elsie, Pip or Lumen now leaves a lasting personal incident with that victim. Their lantern-specific help remains blocked until the aggressor brings two apples and explicitly chooses Make restitution. Ordinary gifts and gradual fear recovery do not erase this incident. Existing broader reputation/fear behavior remains. Peaceful conversation/quest actions remain available while watching; attacking and being damaged through ordinary survival combat do not.
- E can also pick up a reachable current backpack or harvest a ripe tree when no nearby NPC/clue owns the interaction. Pointer/touch interactions retain the same server commands and range checks.

## Authority and storage

The existing room socket binds every command to admitted membership, world/life/zone and target life. The server independently checks a current nearby NPC or the interior recovery anchor with LOS. Fixed `stolen:` action IDs are selectors, not capabilities: the store rechecks recipient, stage, incident, cooldown, inventory revision and capacity. No client price, reward, position or duration is accepted.

SQLite component `stolen-lantern`, version 1, adds `stolen_lantern_state`, `stolen_lantern_personal`, `stolen_lantern_incidents`, `stolen_lantern_rewards` and `stolen_lantern_receipts`. It shares the existing apple inventory/revision. Recovery custody, completion entitlements, claims and restitution are atomic and replay-safe. Real SQLite concurrency/reopen/rollback tests cover duplicate attempts. The enduring incident uses a savepoint inside the existing reputation transaction: any failure rolls back both memories before physical damage. No unsaved incident queue remains.

The new service is created on actual PartyRoom startup and refreshes shared quest/private inventory views on relevant actions and rejoin. Public spirit effect deadlines support prediction/other-player presentation; personal incidents and reward availability remain private. Server simulation and presence remain independent of viewport culling. NPCs, roadside bandits and goblins share one route search per 100ms with alternating priority. Morrow emits at most one pending tell. Rendering uses one bounded spirit graphics object and cached cave/pedestal/goblin textures. No NPC or mob consumes a human room slot.

Deploy additively against the preserved SQLite volume using the release owner’s backup/restart process. Do not replace source or local data. This milestone is stacked on the repaired PR33 and must not be promoted ahead of it.
